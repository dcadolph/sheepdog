Works with what you already use

Your calendars. Your meeting apps.

sheepdog reads every calendar macOS Calendar knows about, and signs in to Microsoft directly for the ones it does not show. When it is time, Join opens the link it found in the invite, straight into the Zoom or Teams app.

Hold my place

Pick up right where you left off.

Type what you were in the middle of before you jump in. When the meeting ends, Tug hands it back. Notes stay on your Mac and clear themselves at 4 a.m. after they are returned.

Get Tug

It only reads. It never writes.

sheepdog reads the calendars already in macOS Calendar and can sign in to Microsoft directly. No server, no account, no analytics. It never creates, changes or deletes an event. Read the privacy policy.
